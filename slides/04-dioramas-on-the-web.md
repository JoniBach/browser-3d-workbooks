## Small worlds on the web

- **Bruno Simon's portfolio** (2019): drive a toy car round a tabletop world. Three.js and Blender; Awwwards Site of the Year
- **Messenger** by Abeto (2025): deliver post on a tiny planet while other players wander past. Three.js, in the browser, on phones too
- Both are **small, finished worlds**: the edge is part of the design
- Low poly and a few colours: quicker to make, and they still read as art

???

Messenger is our workbook 2 grown up: a character walking on a tiny planet. Ours has no
multiplayer and no post, but the idea is the same, and it's 250 lines.

Bruno Simon made his portfolio as an example for his students; it led to his Three.js Journey
course, which a lot of people learn from.

The constraint is the point: a small world can be finished, polished and understood. A whole
real world can't.
