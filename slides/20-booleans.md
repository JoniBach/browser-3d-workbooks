## Thinking in booleans

Solid shapes from simple ones, with three operations:

- **Union**: add shapes together
- **Subtract**: cut one shape out of another
- **Intersect**: keep only where shapes overlap

The keychain: a disc, **union** a tab, **subtract** a hole, **subtract** the grid (trimmed to the disc with **intersect**)

???

This is called constructive solid geometry (CSG). Almost any object you can hold can be built
this way.

The curved grid lines are the same rings as the globe in workbook 1, seen from the front: an
ellipse as wide as the sine of its longitude.
