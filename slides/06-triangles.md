## Everything is triangles

- A **vertex** is a corner: a position in x, y, z
- Three vertices make a **triangle**
- A **normal** says which way a surface faces, so light can shade it
- A sphere is just a lot of small flat triangles
- More triangles: smoother, but more work for the GPU
