## Claude will plan five things with you

- **The world**: what sits on the plinth
- **The focus**: the one thing the eye goes to
- **The motion**: the one thing that moves
- **The palette**: four to seven colours
- **The reveal**: the moment it comes to life

Stuck? Your desk with a steaming coffee · a fox under the northern lights · your home town as
real terrain · a robot dancing on a tiny planet

???

These are the same ideas as the narrative slide earlier: a character or focus, a journey or
motion, one visual language, a reveal. Small worlds work because they're finished.

The ideas are only starters; the best ones come from people's own lives: a holiday, a hobby,
a pet, a place they grew up.
