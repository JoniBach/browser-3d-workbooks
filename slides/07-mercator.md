## Our story: Mercator

- **Gerardus Mercator**, Flemish mapmaker, 1512 to 1594
- **1569**: a world map that sailors could steer by, with straight lines as constant compass bearings
- He coined the word **atlas** for a book of maps
- His projection still runs **every web map** you use
- And it's our logo: a globe drawn with a map's grid lines

???

Our company is named after him, so the talk follows him: his globe, his journeys, his
projection, and finally his name in your pocket.

The Atlas was published in 1595, the year after he died.
