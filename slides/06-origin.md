## Where this came from

- It began as **GT Retrospective**: a motorcycle grand tour, retold as a 3D journey
- Cut down to **geo-globe**: any place on Earth as a diorama
- Cut down again to **these four workbooks**
- Each cut kept the story and lost the weight

???

The adventure motorcycling thread runs right through: the tour itself, and the seat-height line.

The grand tour had the bike, the routes, GPS tracks, photos and stories. geo-globe took all that
out so only the spectacle was left: about 4,000 lines. The workbooks keep one idea each, in
about 200 lines.

This is the same lesson as narrative: decide what the piece is about, and cut everything else.
