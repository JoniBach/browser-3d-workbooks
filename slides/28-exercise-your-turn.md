# Your turn

Make your own small world, in about 20 minutes

- Your idea, on a plinth, in **one HTML file**
- Built with **Claude Code**, using the same four sections
- **No installs**: double-click the file to open it

???

This is the hands-on part. Everyone makes their own diorama: whatever small world they'd like.

What you need: Claude Code, a browser and an internet connection (three.js comes from a CDN).
No npm, no build, no server.

Work in pairs if not everyone has Claude Code.
