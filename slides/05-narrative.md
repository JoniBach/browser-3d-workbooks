## Every small world tells a story

The best three.js pieces aren't tech demos. A theme holds them together:

- **A character** to follow: the car, the postman, our cat
- **A journey**: from somewhere to somewhere, so moving means something
- **One visual language**: a palette, shapes and lines that repeat
- **A reveal**: the moment it comes to life

???

Narrative is what tells you what to leave out. Without it, every feature seems worth adding.

In this talk: the character is the cat, the journey is zooming in from the world to your hand,
the visual language is the logo's grid and gradient (it's in every workbook), and the reveal
is the logo starting to spin in the first minute.
