## Make it yours

Keep asking for changes:

- "Make it night, and light the windows"
- "Add a character that walks round the edge"
- "Use the real hills where I live"
- "Add a slider for the weather"

Then open the file and find **section 2**: that's your world, in code you can read.

No Claude Code? Save **`starter/diorama.html`** from the site and build your world by hand.
