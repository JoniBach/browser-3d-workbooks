## The steps

```sh
git clone https://github.com/JoniBach/browser-3d-workbooks.git
cd browser-3d-workbooks
claude
```

1. Say: **"Help me make a diorama"**
2. Tell it your idea, and answer its questions
3. Check its plan, change anything you like, and let it build
4. Double-click the file it makes in **`my-dioramas/`**

???

The repo's CLAUDE.md tells Claude how to work: ask for your idea, play a short plan back to you,
build from a blank starter, and check the result. It won't decide what you're making for you.

If someone's stuck on step 2, the next slide has ideas.
